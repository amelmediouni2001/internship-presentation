03Problem → solution

Every contribution is verified, not just claimed

Today

Hard to find the right contributors
Owners can't match people to the skills they need.
Progress lives in chats
No structured view of who works on what.
No proof of who did what
Work is spread across repositories and never recognized.

With TalentHub

1
Recruit the right people
Project approved by an admin, CV-based recommendations, applications
2
Work on GitHub / GitLab
Tasks on a Kanban board; starting a task creates its branch
3
Review and verify
Owner approves, then the work is checked against a commit or PR
4
Earn XP and a public portfolio
Verified work becomes visible, even without login
04Requirements

Four actors, five modules, three quality priorities

Actors VisitorContributorProject ownerAdministrator

Functional modules

  1. M1Authentication & usersE-mail or GitHub OAuth login, roles, profile & CV upload
  2. M2Projects & applicationsLifecycle, roles needed, discovery & recommendations
  3. M3Project workspaceTasks, sprints, milestones, Kanban, repositories
  4. M4Review, contributions & portfolioSubmit, review, verify, XP, notifications, showcase
  5. M5Admin back-officeStatistics, moderation, audit logs, settings

Non-functional priorities

Security
JWT (15 min access + 7 day refresh), bcrypt hashing, role guards
Data integrity
Validation on both sides (class-validator, Zod) and relational constraints
Traceability
Audit logs, review history and evidence for every contribution
