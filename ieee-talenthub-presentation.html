02 / Problem

The Problem

Student organizations and development teams face several challenges.

01 Difficult to organize collaborative projects Coordinating multi-member projects across teams is slow and unstructured.
02 Hard to know who contributed No clear visibility into who actually did the work.
03 Contributions are often evaluated manually Time-consuming checks that rarely reflect real effort.
04 Weak student portfolios Portfolios do not reflect the students' real work.
05 No centralized platform No single place to manage projects, teams, tasks and achievements.
